Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical remodel take?
Timeline depends on scope. A bathroom takes 2–6 weeks, a kitchen 4–12 weeks, and a full home remodel or ADU can run 3–12 months. We'll give you a detailed schedule before any work starts.
Do you handle permits?
Yes — permits and inspections are included in every project that requires them. We know San Diego County's permitting process inside and out, and we manage every step so you don't have to.
What's the minimum project size you take on?
We focus on full remodels and construction projects rather than small repairs. Bathroom remodels, kitchen remodels, ADUs, additions, and whole-home projects are our sweet spot. Call us and we'll be straight with you about fit.
Do you offer financing options?
Yes. We work with lenders who offer home improvement and construction loans. We can walk you through options during your free consultation — including HELOCs, ADU-specific financing, and renovation loans.
How much does an ADU cost in San Diego?
ADU costs in San Diego typically range from $150,000 to $400,000+ depending on size, design, and site conditions. Garage conversions tend to be on the lower end. We'll give you a detailed estimate after reviewing your property — no guesswork.
