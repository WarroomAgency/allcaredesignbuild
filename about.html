Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Craftsmanship

Allcare Design/Build was founded with a single conviction: San Diego homeowners deserve a contractor who treats their home with the same care as if it were their own.

For over three decades, we've operated as a true design-build firm — meaning our architects, designers, and construction crews work as one integrated team under one roof. This eliminates the costly gaps between design intent and built reality that plague traditional project delivery.

We've grown from a small remodeling company to one of San Diego County's most trusted full-service design-build contractors, completing more than 2,500 projects ranging from single bathroom refreshes to complete custom home builds — all while maintaining the hands-on, personal service that defines us.

How We Work

The Allcare Design/Build Process

We've refined our process over 35 years to deliver exceptional results while keeping you informed and in control every step of the way.

01

Free Consultation

We visit your home, listen to your vision, and honestly assess what's possible within your budget. No pressure, no obligation — just an expert conversation.

02

Design & Planning

Our in-house design team creates detailed plans and 3D renderings. We handle all permits, material selections, and scheduling before a single nail is driven.

03

Construction

Our skilled crew executes your project with precision. Your dedicated project manager gives you weekly updates and is always reachable — no voicemail black holes.

04

Quality Inspection

Before we call a project done, we conduct a thorough quality inspection and walk-through with you. Every punch-list item is resolved before final payment.

05

Final Walkthrough

We walk through your completed project together, ensuring every detail meets your expectations. We don't close a job until you're 100% satisfied.

06

Ongoing Support

Our relationship doesn't end at move-in. We stand behind our work with comprehensive warranties and remain your trusted contractor for future projects.
