    What Permits Are Needed for an ADU in San Diego?

    An ADU generally requires permits and must comply with applicable state and local building requirements. The exact approvals depend on the property, ADU type, location, design, and applicable jurisdiction.

    California's Department of Housing and Community Development maintains the state's current ADU guidance, and its March 2026 handbook reflects changes to California ADU law that took effect January 1, 2026. Because requirements can change, homeowners should confirm current requirements for their specific property and jurisdiction.

    We handle permit management as part of our design-build process. We coordinate San Diego County permits, inspections, and code compliance, helping homeowners avoid having to manage these steps themselves.
