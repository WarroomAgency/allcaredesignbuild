    Frequently Asked Questions About Our ADU Construction Services

    How much does it cost to build an ADU in San Diego?
    The cost depends on the size, design, property conditions, materials, permits, and type of ADU you choose. We discuss your goals and budget during the consultation and provide a project-specific estimate rather than giving every homeowner the same price.
    How long does ADU construction take?
    Every ADU is different. The timeline depends on design, permitting, project size, materials, and construction requirements. Larger projects that require permits typically take longer to prepare before construction begins. We create a project timeline and keep you updated throughout the process.
    Do you handle ADU permits?
    Yes. We as San Diego ADU builder handle permit management, inspections, and code compliance as part of our design-build service.
    Can you design and build my ADU?
    Yes. We provide both design and construction through one team. From initial planning and 3D renderings to permits, construction, and the final walkthrough, we can manage the complete project.
    Can you convert my existing garage into an ADU?
    Yes. Garage conversions are one of the ADU options we offer. We can evaluate the existing space and help plan the design, permitting, construction, and interior finishing.
